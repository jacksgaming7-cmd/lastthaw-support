Common questions

Where are my save files?

Windows: %APPDATA%\TAH Games\Last Thaw\

worlds\<world>\world.jsonone folder per world: the map state, your character, bases, vehicles and containers
worlds\<world>\world.json.bakautomatic backup, used if the main file is damaged
profile.jsonyour settings, key bindings and name
achievements.jsonthe game's local achievement record

Steam Cloud syncs your worlds and settings. To back up by hand, copy the whole worlds folder.

Playing with friends

Up to 8 players in one world. The host's world is the one everyone plays in.

Steam (easiest): host a world, then invite friends from the Steam overlay (Shift+Tab) or have them right-click you in their friends list → Join Game. Steam relays the connection, so no port forwarding is needed.

Direct IP: the host shares their IP address and the others join it. The host needs UDP port 24567 forwarded to their PC.

Every player needs the same version of the game. Steam keeps everyone up to date automatically.

First launch takes a long time

The first time a world loads, the game builds a terrain cache for the 3.3 km map. That takes a while once, and later loads are much faster.

A slow start every time is usually your antivirus scanning the game files. Adding the game's install folder to your antivirus exclusions normally fixes it.

If the ground ever looks wrong after an update, close the game and delete %APPDATA%\TAH Games\Last Thaw\terrain_cache\. It is rebuilt on the next launch, and your worlds are not touched.

The game won't start, or crashes on launch

Last Thaw needs a graphics card with Vulkan support (most cards from 2016 or later).

1. Update your graphics driver from NVIDIA, AMD or Intel directly. Windows Update drivers often lack Vulkan.
2. Close overlays and recorders you don't need while testing.
3. Still failing? Send us the newest log (below) by email or as a GitHub issue.

Low frame rate or stutter

In Settings, try these in order:

Render scale75% or 67% is the biggest single win on older cards
View distancelower it, especially in the city of Calder
Qualitytakes effect on the next world load
Max FPS / VSynccapping near your monitor's refresh rate smooths small hitches

Big zombie hordes in town are the heaviest moments in the game; that is expected.

My achievements aren't unlocking

Steam has to be running and signed in. If you played offline, the game remembers unlocks and sends them the next time it starts with Steam online.

Last Thaw has 26 achievements. A few are hidden until you earn them.

Voice chat

Hold V to talk to players near you, or hold X to talk on a walkie-talkie if you carry one. Voice only goes to the other players in your world while you hold the key. It is never recorded or saved.

Where do I find my logs?

%APPDATA%\TAH Games\Last Thaw\logs\

Attach the newest file to your bug report. It makes almost every problem faster to find.

Early Access

Last Thaw is being built in public, with major updates every week. Expect rough edges: balance is still being tuned, parts of the new land (Calder, the airport and the Military Base) are still filling in, and the vehicle line-up is being redesigned. Your feedback on GitHub or by email decides what gets fixed first.
